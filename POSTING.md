# Posting times

3 videos a day on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube. Times are German time (Europe/Berlin), from
Metricool "best time to post" (week of 2026-10-26). The account was connected on 2026-10-21, so
these are Metricool's general values, not yet our own audience: check again after about 20 posts.

| Slot | TikTok | Instagram Reels | YouTube Shorts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10:00 | 10:00 | 10:00 |
| 2 | 15:00 | 13:00 | 13:00 |
| 3 | 18:00 | 18:00 | 16:00 |

- Each slot is a peak hour with at least 3 hours between posts, so a new video does not take the
  first push away from the one before.
- Best days: Wednesday and Thursday (Tuesday and Friday close). Put the strongest video of the
  week in a Wednesday or Thursday slot.
- Weekends reach about half as many people. Keep posting at the same times, with the weaker videos.
- Same video on all three platforms in the same slot.
- Timing helps a little. The hook in second 1 and watching to the end decide whether it travels.
