# Swipe file: October 2026

Source: YouTube (Shorts ≤ 70 s), read with `yt-dlp` on 2026-10-26. Raw data:
`top-shorts-2026-10.tsv` (most-viewed shorts by topic) and `niche-shorts-2026-10.tsv`
(recent shorts of 15 small stickman / motivation channels, with each channel's median).

## How sure is this?

- **Mega shorts (top-shorts):** 31 videos, ranked by raw views. These are the biggest shorts in
  "animation", "life lesson", "motivation" and "money"; most are kids' animation. Raw views are
  not outlier multiples (no channel medians were taken for these big channels), so they show
  **which formats can travel**, not what beats a channel's normal day.
- **Niche (niche-shorts):** 350 shorts from 15 small channels, ranked by outlier multiple. The
  channels are tiny (medians 7–16,000 views), so the outliers are hints, not findings.
- Hooks are taken from titles (these animations rarely speak), not from on-screen text.

## Formats that travel (from the mega shorts)

| Formula | Example (views) | Our version |
|---|---|---|
| **Simple lesson with one object** | "Ellie's Coin Jar: A Lesson on Saving Money" (567M) · "Ice Cream: A Lesson in Line Etiquette" (2.3B) | One person, one object, one money lesson a child understands |
| **X vs Y / good vs bad** | "Man vs Cartoon Characters" (1.2B) · "What is GOOD and what is BAD?" (145M) | Two choices side by side (spend vs save, scroll vs build) |
| **Seamless loop** | "Watering pants instead of plants #loop" (272M, 12 s) | The end cuts back into the start, so people watch it twice (e.g. the payday cycle) |
| **Satisfying transformation** | "Sausage is cut into ice creams" (242M, 12 s) | Something small turns into something big (coin → snowball → fortune) |
| **Relatable daily problem** | "Every night problem" (231M) · "Why I shouldn't sleep in class" (126M) | "Payday vs day 3", "me after buying something on pay later" |
| **Emotional twist, stickman** | Alan Becker, "Bob Is Always There" (66M) | Who stays when you have nothing |
| **Warning hook** | "Don't do this" (283M, 8 s) | "Don't do this with your salary." |

What they share: one idea per video, no reading needed to follow, the picture tells the joke or the
lesson, the payoff is visual, and many are short enough (8–30 s) to be watched again.

## What to make next

1. **Payday loop** (relatable + loop): payday feels rich, day 3 broke, day 29 waiting, and
   payday again. The loop is the trap; one person steps out of it.
2. **The snowball** (satisfying transformation + numbers): $100 a month rolls down a mountain
   and grows to $149,000 in 30 years; the one who starts 10 years late gets far less.
3. **Big number rise and fall**: "$70 million to bankrupt", told with stickmen, no names.
