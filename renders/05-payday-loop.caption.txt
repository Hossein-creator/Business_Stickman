Payday feels rich, day 29 feels broke. Save this if every month ends the same way.

Day 1: salary in, dinners out, new shoes. Day 29: instant noodles and an empty account.

Without a budget, the paycheck to paycheck cycle runs like a hamster wheel. The months change, the month stays the same.

The way out is a plan on payday: savings first, bills second, spending last.

A typical month, not anyone's real account.
