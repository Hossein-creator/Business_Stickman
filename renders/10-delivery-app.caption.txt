Your $12.50 delivery order, split three ways. Save this before your next checkout.

The restaurant keeps about $8.00 after a 20% commission. The rider keeps $1.60 of the delivery fee. The app keeps $2.90, about 23% of what you paid.

It owns no kitchen and no scooter. It owns the connection between them.

That is how a food delivery app makes money, and why platform business models grow so fast.

Example numbers. Fees change by app and city.
