How a gym makes money from people who never come. Save this before you sign up.

A gym with 1,000 members at $20 a month takes in $20,000 every month. It only has room for about 100 people at a time.

Where your $20 goes: rent $4, staff $3, machines $3, power and water $2, cleaning and other costs $2. That leaves $6 profit per member, or $6,000 a month.

This gym business model only works because most members stop coming and keep paying. In 2014 NPR reported that Planet Fitness gyms had about 6,500 members each and room for around 300.

A membership only pays off if you show up. Same for every subscription you pay each month.

Example gym, rough numbers. Real gyms differ by city and size.
