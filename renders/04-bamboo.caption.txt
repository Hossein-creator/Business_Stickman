He watered it for 4 years and nothing grew. Save this for the days your work shows no results.

For four years the bamboo seed shows nothing above ground, and people laugh.

Under the ground it grows roots the whole time. Then the bamboo shoots up about 27 meters in 6 weeks.

Building a business, savings or a skill works the same way: slow roots first, fast growth later.

A well known story, growth depends on the type of bamboo.
