Investing $100 every month at an 8% average yearly return grows to about $149,036 in 30 years.

Start 10 years later with the same $100 a month and you end with about $58,902.

That gap is compound interest. Most of the money comes from time, not from the amount.

Example only: 8% average yearly return, not guaranteed, before fees and taxes.
