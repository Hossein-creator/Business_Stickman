Got $30? Start a shoe cleaning business this weekend. Save this business idea for Saturday.

The kit: brushes $8, cleaner $10, cloths $5, bucket $3, a sign $4. Total $30.

Clean one pair of sneakers for $5. Cleaner and cloth cost about $0.40, so you keep $4.60 a pair.

Eight pairs on a Saturday is $36.80. Only weekends, 5 hours a day, 8 days a month: about $294 a month from a $30 start. A small side hustle with no shop and no loan.

Example prices. Rent, permits and slow days not included. Check local prices and rules first.
