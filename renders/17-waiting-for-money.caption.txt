Waiting for $10,000 to start a business? Save this for the day you think you are not ready.

Two people, the same three years. One sits and waits for the perfect amount of starting capital. The other starts with $10 of iced tea on a crate.

Month 6: the crate turns into a cart. Year 3: the cart turns into a small tea house. The one who waited is still saving and still not ready.

You do not need a lot of money to start a small business. You need a first customer, then the next one.

A story, not a promise. Most small businesses grow slower than this.
