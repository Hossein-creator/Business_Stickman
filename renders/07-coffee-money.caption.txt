Your $5 coffee, split into every cost. Save this the next time you think a cafe is easy money.

Beans $0.35, milk $0.30, cup and lid $0.25, rent $1.10, staff $1.60, power, machine and fees $0.80.

That leaves about $0.60 profit per latte. To make $3,000 a month, a small cafe has to sell about 167 cups every single day.

That is how a coffee shop makes money: thin margins and a lot of volume.

Example numbers for a small cafe. Real costs vary by city.
