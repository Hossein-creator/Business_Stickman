You pay $4 for bubble tea. Here is what the shop keeps. Save this before you open a drink shop.

Tapioca pearls $0.25, tea and milk $0.40, sugar syrup $0.10, cup, seal and straw $0.25. Rent $1.00, staff $0.90, power, machines and fees $0.40.

That leaves about $0.70 profit per cup. To make $3,000 a month, a small bubble tea shop has to sell about 143 cups every single day.

That is how a bubble tea shop makes money: cheap ingredients, expensive rent and staff, and a lot of cups.

Example small shop at $4 a cup. Real costs vary by city.
