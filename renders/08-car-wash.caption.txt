Got $50? Start a car wash this weekend. Save this business idea for Saturday.

The kit: bucket $8, car soap $10, sponges $7, microfiber cloths $15, spray $10. Total $50.

Wash a car for $10. Six cars a day is $60, minus a $5 soap refill, so you keep $55.

Only on weekends, 8 days a month, that is about $440 a month from a $50 start. A real side hustle with no shop and no loan.

Example prices. Check local prices and rules first.
