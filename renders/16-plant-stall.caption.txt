Got $60? Grow plants and sell them for $3 each. Save this business idea for your next weekend.

The kit: potting soil $25, cuttings and seeds $15, a tray $10, a watering can $5, labels and a sign $5. Total $60. Pots are free: recycled cups and tins.

Cuttings take about 3 weeks to root. Each plant costs about $0.70 in soil, cutting and label, so you keep $2.30 when you sell it for $3.

Sell 12 plants on a Saturday and that is $27.60. Only weekends, 8 days a month: about $220 a month from a $60 start. A small side hustle you can start from home.

Example prices. Rent, permits and slow days not included. Check local prices and rules first.
