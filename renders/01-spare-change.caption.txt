$5 a day for one year: spend it or keep it. Save this before you buy your next snack.

Two people get the same $5 every day. One spends it right away. One drops it in a jar first.

After 365 days the first one has receipts. The second one has $1,825, enough to start a small iced tea cart.

That is the pay yourself first habit: put money aside before you spend, then let small savings become starting capital for a business.

Simple example, no interest included.
