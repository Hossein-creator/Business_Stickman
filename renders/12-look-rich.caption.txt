Same $100 a month. One buys a phone on installments, one buys a business. Save this for your next payday.

Year 1: one pays $100 a month for a $1,200 phone. The other saves $100 a month in a jar.

After 12 months the phone is worth about $500. The jar buys a small coffee cart.

Year 2: a new phone model comes out and the installments start again. The cart brings in around $300 a month, $3,600 in the year.

Looking rich costs money every month. Getting rich starts with one thing you own that pays you back.
